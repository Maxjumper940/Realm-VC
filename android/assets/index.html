    About Realm VC

    A group voice call for up to 10 people that runs only on your phone. It does not connect to a Minecraft Realm, a Minecraft server, or any game. The mic stays at one level: it is not turned up on its own, and a limiter keeps a loud moment from clipping.

    1. Allow the microphone when asked.
    2. Create a room and share the short code.
    3. Up to nine other people join with that code.
