Phone group call

REALM VC

A 3-person voice call that runs only on your phone. It does not connect to a Minecraft Realm, a Minecraft server, or any game.

  1. Allow the microphone when asked.
  2. Create a room and share the short code.
  3. Up to two other people join with that code.

Add Realm VC to your Home Screen

It'll open like a normal app, with its own icon.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then open Realm VC from your Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

Or get the Android APK.
